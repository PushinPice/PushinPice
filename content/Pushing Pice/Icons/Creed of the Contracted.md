Controlling and Conniving
### Icon: The Concordist
![[Pasted image 20260514225954.png]]

A faction that works in the shadows, the Creed works tirelessly day and night to enlist people in their agenda. What agenda is that? Signing their lives away for one deed or another! There's a fiendish air about this cult. They're always scheming finding the best time to pitch a deal to you at your lowest circumstances. And it's always such creepy blabbering "Blah 1 give you power blah give me your soul". In some ways, they seem crazy and harmless. But you really have to wonder: how high does their conspiracy climb?

**Quote:** "Why put in all that work... with a swish of a pen, you can have all you've ever dreamed off All it takes is a soul"

**Typical Location:** When not operating in the shadows it is said that the scarlet crimson towers in the Great Glacier hold the Creed's leadership.

**Common Knowledge:** In general, the Creed only pursues people who are rumored to be at their lowest. Divorce terminal disease, desperate folk...all are likely targets for the Creed to recruit. But cults are typically not supernatural. The Creed feels different. Their symbols are devoted to old fiendish, origins, Rumor has it that anyone who has attended one of their cultish rituals has been forced to swear oaths of secrecy. How do we know this? Those who revealed details have vanished into thin air, leaving nothing but smoke and the smell of sulfur in their wake. One has to wonder: is it just the common folk affected? Many suspect that at least one Icon may be involved with them behind closed doors. It that's the case, then the Creed and Concordist must have a plan involving the continent!

**Adventurer Quests:** Those wrapped up with the Creed can find themselves in a world of trouble. Maybe they're trying to help out a poor soul trapped in a sticky situation. Or perhaps they've made a deal of their own.. either trying to get out or follow through on whatever deed was promised. If things get bad enough, it's possible to get...extraplanar. So many rumors have circulated about fiends and the Nine Hells. Would mortal ideas of this rumored terrible plane capture how monstrous a quest here could be? If adventurers are not keen to find out, then it's probably best that they curb any progress the Creed makes immediately!
### Icon Relationships:
**Positive:** [[Sainthood of Six]]. Fiendish activity indicates Onir, Man O' War. The Sainthood sees the Creed as a possible last remnant of the gods.
**Negative:** [[Elemental Emergence]]. They compete for humanoid dominance, What could be more disruptive to the elementals symbiotic relationship than corrupting mortal souls!
**Conflicted:** [[The Rotwalkers]]. The Creed finds them odd. Undead are husks, no soul to speak of. Not worthy of pursuing, yet a possible source of competition when engaging with humanoids.
