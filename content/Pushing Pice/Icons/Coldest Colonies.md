Independent and Growing
### Icon: Governor Talbot
![[Pasted image 20260514225743.png]]

Governor Talbot, a Tiefling with snow- white hair and even crisper formal robes, leads this faction on the brink of wilderness. 75 years ago, they were colonies of the Diamond Dynasty of Fien. Guerilla warfare and the aid of Frost Giants led to a new state of Independence for these colonies. They've maintained their land ever since...but the time is nigh to expand territory! Will you help the Colonies in their adventurous endeavor into the cold wilderness? Or will you work against them and aid their enemies?

**Quote:** "The world is a cold place, and that's how we like it, Ice preserves...it's meant to last eons. Other forces interfere with this fact. They'll have to pry it from our cold, living, hands."
Typical Location: the Governor resides in Stiria, capital of the colonies. His people have settlements across the frozen forests of the north.

**Common Knowledge:** Compared to the rest of the continent, the Colonies are remote and isolated. Winter is cold and punishing. However, people here have learned to prosper. Hunters and rangers are prominent, as well as practitioners of cold elemental magic. Rumor has it that the Governor has secured alliances with monsters of ice...what a way to keep the Colonies protected! Colonists place high belief in their Governor's goals: not only to protect, but also to expand their territory. This is where other influences play a role. Colonists are deeply scared of "Rotwalkers" that wander in the cold darkness. What else could stand in the way of their land expansion??

**Adventurer Quests:** Those who align with denizens of the Colonies often are involved in what they hold dearest: cold and colonization. Perhaps adventurers are interested in helping to foster new elemental pacts on behalf of the Colonies. Expeditions are always in need of additional hands to establish the groundwork for new villages. The colonists are superstitious folk; adventurers can expect plenty of requests to hunt down enemies that seem dangerous at a glance. But colonists likely need just as much protection against hidden threats.
### Icon Relationships:
**Positive:** [[Elemental Emergence]]. Elementals of ice and Water have made a symbiotic relationship with the Coldest Colonies across the frozen North. 
**Negative:** [[The Rotwalkers]]. Nothing is scarier for a colonist than the thought of an undead wandering in the cold night. How do they end up in the same territory so consistently?
**Conflicted:** [[Fien's Foreigners]]. Fien's Diamond Dynasty previously owned the Colonies. Peace is here...but convergence of these icons makes that peace fragile...
