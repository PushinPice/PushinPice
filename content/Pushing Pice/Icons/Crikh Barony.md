Head in the Clouds
### Icon: Baron Nasim V
![[Pasted image 20260514230348.png]]

The Barony controls the single largest collective territory on this continent. It's sad to say that its mostly barren desert...but that doesn't stop the Barony from thriving. Terrestrial oases,
underground water sources, and the River Phiaro have allowed for the country to thrive. None more so than the capital city of Minzar. Its grand heights are only matched by the efficient chutes that make it the most bustling centralized city on the continent. Even with all this to boast of, the Baron seems bored. He'd much rather keep his eyes to the stars than glued to the ground. Everyone wonders what he longs for amidst that night sky.

**Quote:** "I will not retire to my chambers as an old man who accomplished nothing. Those stars out there...they were meant to be held by the hands of my people!"

**Typical Location:** The desert holds most of the Barony's denizens. In Minzar, the Baron keeps himself mostly confined to the Opulent Observatory.

**Common Knowledge:** The Baron is a "hands-off" ruler. He delegates well, allowing for his cabinet to handle most of the day-to-day duties of maintaining his vast desert territory. If needed, he keeps a council chamber for public testimony and decision-making on urgent issues.
His heart lies most deeply with the stars above. Fien's Foreigners convinced him that secrets lie in their darkness...so he has invested in magical technology. Rumors circulate that the Baron has made a breakthrough regarding the beautiful Twin Moons! But with his head in the sky, other icons could take advantage of the Baron's lofty ambitions.

**Adventurer Quests:** The Baron's many servants may need aid in their quest to maintain peace with other unruly icons. But adventurers could just as easily get caught up in the Baron's quest to explore the stars. Becoming privy to magical secrets or balancing the push and pull of power are likely motivations to involve adventurers.
### Icon Relationships:
**Positive:** [[Fien's Foreigners]]. The Baron has a close relationship with the Arcane Loremaster, who has helped the Barony advance their civilization fast.
**Negative:** [[Fiefdom Afloat]]. Their kidnapping tendencies distracts from any lofty research. Unacceptable from the Barony's perspective.
**Conflicted:** [[Sainthood of Six]]. Religion is not as well-trusted in this land. But the Saints have power, enough to earn a place at the table when the Barony makes decisions.
