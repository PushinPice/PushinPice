Secrets and Slavery
### Icon: The All-Seer
![[Pasted image 20260514231413.png]]
There is not a single person who can understate this fact: the Fiefdom is the WORST place alive for any humanoid to live. Other regions may have harsher elements, but the Fiefdom's focus on slavery means the environment is changed in a different way. People are taken to their floating islands and never seen again...or if they are, they are entirely changed personality-wise. People are plucked from the land below, the seas to the side, and even the skies above. Nobody is spared. Everyone wonders the same things: how are they using these slaves, and who is this All-Seer behind their kidnappings? Answers are minimal, which is a problem as the Fiefdom targets more noteworthy humanoids for capture.

**Quote:** "Eye for an eye eh? You're unlucky: I have many eyes to spare. I see so much. All my workers toiling away. All for me. It gives me joy to see this present...almost as much as the future I envision."

**Usual Location:** Slaves and slavers live atop the Floating Isles. Sometimes the wander to the surface, always causing trouble. The All-Seer's location isn't so clear. Some question if he even is physically on the islands at all.

**Common Knowledge:** As mentioned, they target stragglers who lurk around their floating isles. No race or faction is exempt...except strangely for the Ocean's Conqueror. Captured seafarers have given details that slaves are put to work on the isle, building intricate temples on the fertile land. Taskmasters are rewarded with higher ranks for obedience. Only the highest ranked are given access to interact with the All-Seer. They come back changed from the experience; glassy eyes, personality shifts, and more have been noted. The All-Seer himself? Never seen. Some doubt if he's even real. But nobody doubts his influence. Rumors swirl of his increasingly targeted kidnapping attempts. Could an icon be at risk from these heinous crimes?

**Adventurer Quests:** Quests often involve rescue or prevention of kidnapping against the Fiefdom. Little is known about the isle...making research a potential source of interest. If adventurers are unlucky, or perhaps evil, they may partake in aiding the Fiefdom in their enslavement mission. From either side, the true achievement would be to lay eyes on the All-Seer in his true form.

### Icon Relationships:
**Positive:** [[Ocean's Conqueror]]. They have a strange deal in place...they pillage the seaside, and the Fiefdom gets the stragglers. Truly a business arrangement.
**Negative:** [[Crikh Barony]]. With the Baron distracted, his people become an easy target. Especially as they are desperate for water in the desert.
**Conflicted:** [[Ushi, the Grand Plutocracy]]. While the danger of enslavement stays true, Ushi is able to placate the worst kidnappings with material goods exchanges.
