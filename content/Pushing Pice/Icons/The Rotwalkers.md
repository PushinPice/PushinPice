Isolated Unlife
### Icon: Maker of Dust
![[Pasted image 20260514233032.png]]

Dead Man Walking. That's the easiest way to describe the Rotwalkers. They are by far the most isolated of the icons, making them also the most misunderstood. Other Icons have an understandable fear of necromancy. Everyone fears death...and to have it mirrored in the form of clacking skeletons or zombified husks shoves that fear in everyone's face. Nobody knows this better than the Coldest Colonies. Frequent encounters with undead wandering the frozen north has convinced them of two important revelations. Firstly: the undead must have a stronghold amidst the snows. Secondly: their behavior patterns display a level of intelligence. All signs point towards the seemingly "mindless" undead being led by an organized leader. His true name is unknown, but his rumored feats of necromancy have earned him his title: Maker of Dust. As with all undead, he must have been a person in life! The question remains: does he carry the same goals as his living mind once did? Or has timeless existence changed his vision to achieve stranger heights? The only clear answer is that death is his tool of choice in this journey.

**Quote:** "Life? Life is fragile, fleeting, and so damn restricted. Death is formless...timeless...and gives a chance beyond life. Everyone who wants a chance at it should have it. There is no compromise between life and death."

**Usual Location:** The Maker of Dust is found within the Fortress City of Sterility, where all undead house their sunlight sensitive bodies. Past sundown, or during weather like a blizzard, Rotwalkers can be found wandering past their borders.

**Common Knowledge:** Given their isolation, much of the rumors surround the reclusive nature of the Rotwalkers. Some spies of the Colonies have spotted that their fortress is covered by day...but unveils to show strange architecture constructed of bone. That is Sterility, fortress of the Rotwalkers. Its unsettling sight is magnified by what the undead do outside of their settlement. Zombified corpses and rattling skeletons have been caught wandering in dark blizzards, encroaching on humanoid territories to conduct odd rituals or grave rob in the night. The uncomfortable nature of these encounters has led to skirmishes; undead are not particularly communicative to quell fear. They likely get this from their leader: Maker of Dust has shown little interest in parlaying with the outside world. Some say that the Deep Dwellers have laid claim to successfully earning the Maker's attention. What could they possibly have in common?

**Adventurer Quests:** If coming from the Colonies, quests often involve quelling undead interaction or disrupting strange rituals. The Creed seems to have a vested interest in gaining more insight into the Rotwalkers' society. Adventurers lucky enough, or perhaps unlucky, to pierce the veil of Sterility may find themselves at the mercy of the Maker. Only those privy to his ear have performed any quests...and certainly not in the public eye. Adventurers can expect that death is involved in the equation if the Rotwalkers are stirring the pot.
### Icon Relationships:
**Positive:** [[Dwellers of the Deep]]. Is it the shared sunlight sensitivity, or feeling of outcast that keep their bond strong? Perhaps they fit like a glove on this continent.
**Negative:** [[Coldest Colonies]]. It's a safe bet that superstitious colonists find great unease in the undead presence. Especially when they snatching up bodies for reasons unknown.
**Conflicted:** [[Creed of the Contracted]]. Working behind the scenes leads to unknown variables. Are these two factions in competition for the valuable resource of living souls?
