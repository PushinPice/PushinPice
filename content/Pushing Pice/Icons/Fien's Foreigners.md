Progress by Proxy
### Icon: The Arcane Loremaster
![[Pasted image 20260514231945.png]]
Science, secrets, and expungement of gods...that is the way of Fien. The 13 divine entities that once ruled supreme have been relegated to irrelevancy thanks to the Loremaster. This doctrine has been replaced by absolute faith in science; a faith that united Fien under a single banner. With that continent known and controlled, the Loremaster's attention turned to the land across the sea. Perhaps this place can drive new inventions and adventure! Yet a smaller sect of Godly folk remains a thorn: Six Saints in particular. The Sainthood has spread rumors far and wide, stopping Fien's science from taking a strong foothold with most Icons. Except for the Baron! His goals and personality align well with the Loremaster's taste. Fien has its work cut out to overcome the natural distaste from the Sainthood to drive science to its peak.

**Quote:** "I conquered our lands with my wits and magical secrets, freeing us from bowing to barbaric gods. Your continent would benefit so much from our values. Yet I fear forces beyond the world may grind that progress to a halt."

**Usual Location:** The Loremaster bounces between the Barony and his home in Vikkurst. Other agents of Fien find themselves traveling the continent in search of archeological sites or new additions to their scientific doctrine.

**Common Knowledge:** When Foreigners are encountered, one can expect intellectualism to reign. supreme. Some prefer classic wizardry, while others may enjoy tinkering. Their locations reflect this, such as artificers flocking to Mt. Wela for dwarven crafting. However, the Sainthood has forced Foreigners to operate with subtlety. Areas rife with godly folk make science an easy target; rumors circulate that the first "Great Offering" to the Fiefdom was a group of Fien's folk captured by radicals. But where Fien's influence thrives, places shine. Minzar's desert capital is an excellent example...with its many chutes and telescopes due to Fien's hard work. Not even the Sainthood could twist this feat. Yet those close to the Loremaster know he is troubled. He has revealed little publicly, but tall tales of strange monsters and odd planar sightings are his points of interest. What does he hypothesize will happen on this continent?!

**Adventurer Quests:** When it comes to advancing science, Fien will take all the help it can get! Or perhaps adventurers have a taste for ruining the Sainthood's reputation. The older-lived species still remember the days when the Diamond Dynasty owned the Coldest Colonies. Perhaps old wounds are reopened and in need of treatment.

### Icon Relationships:
**Positive:** [[Crikh Barony]]. The Baron owes his favorite hobby to Fien. They work seamlessly together to push the limits of spells and stars.
**Negative:** [[Sainthood of Six]]. They are PISSED about being shoved out of Fien. All blame at the gods' lacking presence is blamed on the Loremaster's actions.
**Conflicted:** [[Coldest Colonies]]. From owned to independent, they find it to be like seeing an ex in public. Is there any leftover animosity from the break?
