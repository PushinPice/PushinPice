An Unlikely Alliance
### Icon: The Tribal Trifecta
![[Pasted image 20260514232542.png]]
Three great leaders keep the Radiant Commune united under its vibrant landscape. No other country can lay claim to a mix of volcanic mountains, beautiful tropics, and cultural architecture across the coast. Three main races of high elves, mountain dwarves, and orcs reflect this diversity. For a long time, this land was harmonious. Sure, there were small scuffles here and there! But nothing that was a long-lasting threat...until the Conqueror came. Statues of old heroes and temples dedicated to natural beauty crumpled in the face of this ravaging lunatic. It was disaster that the fragmented societies couldn't oppose. A quarter-century has passed since they united: all thanks to a major deal made with The Grand Plutocracy. The Plutocracy provides rare earth materials while the Commune's fertile soil gets them all the food they need to feed a country. How do they take advantage of this? By pumping all their efforts into crafting rare hardware or natural magic of course! Their goal is to make major moves against the Conqueror's perceived weak points...but the question is whether or not this union is strong enough to strike.

**Quote:** "Races long divided now interlock arms against a common enemy. So much strength in a single union! If only we could decide on a direction. It's hard to succeed when we all want different things..."

**Common Knowledge:** Everyone knows that the Commune and Plutocracy share a deep bond. Regular trade routes have been negotiated between them, and these trade routes feed important materials to the tribal centralizations. Mt. Wela is the ultimate dwarven forge, harnessing the volcano's lava to rework the rare materials. Haughty elves have taken refuge in the Obsidian Hills after their previous Icon was petrified by the Conqueror...they aim to rebuild their old glory in. Lastly, the Orcs live a nomadic life at sea. They constantly hunt for their invader, keeping all important goods aboard their armada at all times. This union is desperately trying to hold themselves together. Not only is the Conqueror striking along the fertile coastline, the Commune is being approached by another suitor: the Deep Dwellers. They claim to have resources that edge out even the Plutocracy's deep pockets. The Commune's country is at stake with such fragmented pieces...can they put it together?

**Adventurer Quests:** With each of the Trifecta having unique traits, quests tend to fit their interests. Dwarves may need to harness the deep sections of their volcano for forging, while the High Elves seek to negotiate with the Deep Dwellers to rebuild their home. The Orcs always need new hands on deck in their expeditions to new islands that may harbor the conqueror. Getting them all to get along is truly the ultimate crux in these quests.
Usual Location: The Icons and their emissaries reside in their three tribes' primary locales: the Obsidian Hills, the fiery Mt. Wela, and Paint Splatter Isle. That encompasses the High Elves, Mountain Dwarves, and Orcs respectively.
### Icon Relationships:
**Positive:** [[Ushi, the Grand Plutocracy]]. Little farming is needed with the Commune providing all fruits of labor. Rare materials are gladly given in return to this shaky union.
**Negative:** [[Ocean's Conqueror]]. He singlehandedly drove the High Elves from their home and destroyed their Icon. He's a threat to do it again to the others.
**Conflicted:** [[Dwellers of the Deep]]. For a desperate country, these could be helpful allies. Or maybe they're being manipulated into taking false gifts? It's hard to tell for the Commune.
