Layers and Layers
### Icon: The Spider Queen
![[Pasted image 20260514230917.png]]
Unknowable levels of cave systems lie below the world's surface. Very few mortals know how widespread these underground tunnels spread...but their influence is present in just about every surface-level landscape. The Spider Queen, a lady of secrecy and expansive plots, spins a web of Underdark denizens into doing her bidding. Dark Drow, brutish Duergar, and many more humanoids count themselves among her influence. They seem to be inserting that influence onto the surface more and more. If the Spider Queen has such an intricate web of control over the Underdark, what could she possibly want by spreading to the Icons Above?

**Quote:** "Silk is strong and beautiful, like our lady. Yet we feel secluded. Unrecognized. A web with no visitors makes the spider starve. We'll get our recognition...we're hungry for it."

**Typical Location:** The Citadel of Silk houses the rumored Spider Queen. She spins her webs far and wide, allowing her denizens to be present anywhere the surface meets the Underdark.

**Common Knowledge:** If anything sticks, it's the knowledge that the Underdark denizens struggle with sunlight. Any interaction with them on the surface has to be done at night. In the ground? Well that's their territory...
Everyone asks the same question: why are they coming to the surface? Very few have held true interactions with the Dwellers. Rumors circulate that they aren't necessarily hostile. Just shy! Members of the Radiant Commune indicate that, when negotiating, the Dwellers have much to offer the surface world. Strange flora and food, rare gemstones and artifacts, and so much more. Great for some...but causes other icons to be threatened. They question the generosity of the Spider Queen's minions; could they have an ulterior motive to their bartering?!

**Adventurer Quests:** the Dwellers are just as likely to send you deep into their caverns as they are to sprawl the surface. Perhaps they need a mediator to be present for forging a new icon relationship. Or maybe they need haughty adventurers to guard a convoy that delves into a rare Underdark locale. Adventurers can expect to always encounter the unfamiliar when asked for a quest.
### Icon Relationships:
**Positive:** [[The Rotwalkers]]. Two factions with sunlight sensitivity? Common ground makes for quick allies on this continent.
**Negative:** [[Ushi, the Grand Plutocracy]]. Nothing makes Ushi angrier than competition over rare commodities. 
**Conflicted:** [[Radiant Commune of Mennatos]]. Their need for raw materials puts them in a tricky situation regarding dealing with Ushi versus the Dwellers Below.
