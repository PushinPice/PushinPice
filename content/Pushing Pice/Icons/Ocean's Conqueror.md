Living to Crush
### Icon: Ocean's Conqueror
![[Pasted image 20260514232322.png]]

An icon, a beast, and a brute all rolled into one. Smart in the ways of war, and mystical in his connection to the ocean. He IS the Ocean's Conqueror. He and his disciples terrorize the Western shores of the continent, doing the most damage to the Radiant Commune's daily life. They raid and set off into the ocean's waves after their razing is complete. If it were only his disciples, then resistance would be manageable. Most of their basic encampments have been pushed back by organized forces. It get tricky when the Conqueror arrives. He is a hurricane on the battlefield. When he fights, you LOSE. The question everyone asks is simple: why?! His targets do not seem to have a clear pattern. Keeping his wave of destruction at bay will involve solving this puzzle.

**Quote:** "I ain't playin'. You have exactly what I need, and you know exactly the kind of carnage that comes with my presence. I'll give you 12 hours to waive the white flag."

**Usual Location:** The Conqueror loves riding his beautiful flagship, the Europa. His cronies are often camped out on the Western shorelines.

**Common Knowledge:** Very few have met the Conqueror and lived to tell the tale. They all report the same traits: he is fair, cold, confusing, ruthless, and agitated. He seems...impatient? And his strike zones have made little to no sense from an outside perspective. He is just as likely to conquer a spree of random islands as he is to disappear for months at a time at sea. Perhaps somebody could make sense of his patterns, if any exist. Very few are willing to try after the last Icon incident. The Europa still holds the ex-Elf Queen Everflame's petrified body as her figurehead. The Radiant Commune is pissed, but other icons are unwilling to work against him. After all, he's rarely attacking central locales. Who cares if he raids or randomly disappears for long stretches? The Commune pushes their suspicions though; any Icon that seems to be on solid terms with the Fiefdom while wielding such elemental power clearly has nefarious intentions.

**Adventurer Quests:** Working against the Conqueror likely requires managing a surprise attack, either for or against forces. Adventurers question what sort of quests the Icon himself would ask for. Aside from raiding, all eyes turn towards the sea.

### Icon Relationships:
**Positive:** [[Fiefdom Afloat]]. They seem to have a deal, where the conquered end up atop the floating isles if nearby.
**Negative:** [[Radiant Commune of Mennatos]]. A destroyed Icon and pillaged shoreline has led to a LOT of animosity. It brought a whole new alliance in their country!
**Conflicted:** [[Elemental Emergence]]. Strong connection with water makes it complex. Is water meant to be so vicious? Or would elementals bring out another side of the Conqueror?
