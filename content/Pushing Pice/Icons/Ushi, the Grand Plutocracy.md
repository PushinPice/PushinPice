The Chase is Decadent
### Icon: Green Thumb
![[Pasted image 20260514233544.png]]
MONEY. Rare gems. Crystals and gold. The more you have, the better life gets in the Grand Plutocracy. It's a numbers game! Those at the top dominate those below them, where cash is king and makes all law. Merchants from here travel the world to tout their rare gemstones. In return? They bring back necessities, like food and raw materials, to keep this endless money-machine moving. The Commune is their favorite customer for a reason! It's said that this partnership was forged by Ushi's most idolized merchant: the Icon Green Thumb. Rarely spotted, yet unbelievably impactful. That's how Green Thumb operates. While the remaining merchants scheme the surface. Most merchants abide by the codes of their deals in the Twin Citadels within Ushi...Green Thumb bides his time for bigger fish to fry. But despite his immense reputation, even the most rabid supporters of the Plutocracy have doubts. Clear competition, like the Deep Dwellers, place the wealth and reputation of Ushi at risk. They need a new, shiny, deal with an Icon...will Green Thumb live up to his legendary sales skillset?

**Quote:** "The flow of gold is limited...and more hands add to the pile every day. Nobody wants to make labor their life's love. We want GLAMOUR. We want GLORY. We want everything Green Thumb has to offer us."

**Usual Location:** Most merchants, when not traveling, lie in either the Citadel of Crystal or the Citadel of Gold. Green Thumb? Some say he lives in his own abode deep in the Silent Swamps of Ushi's massive wilderness.

**Common Knowledge:** Two Citadels rule this country. One of gold, supplying the coinage craved across the continent. The other of crystal, providing the gemstones involved in art, technology, and rare magic. Both compete for the best merchants and the attention of Green Thumb. But both align in their dire need to maintain and seek new alliances. Especially with the Radiant Commune. Their strongest ally provides the majority of food and raw materials to allow for this Plutocracy to remain functional. However, there is strife beyond the usual bickering between citadels. The Deepest Dwellers are rumored to have their own fresh and fancy jewels to compete with Ushi's dominance on the market. Everyone also wonders about Green Thumb's absence...is he even alive, or one man alone? It's said that some merchants are making their own wild moves thanks to his silence.

**Adventurer Quests:** Ushi will make a deal with just about anyone. Often involving movement of money or other goods, you can bet that adventurers will find themselves entrapped with merchants of all sorts. But the Deep Dwellers may find themselves inserting in any deal seeming good enough to be worth ascending to the surface. Most merchants abide by the codes of the Plutocracy...yet a rogue tradesman is likely to make for interesting disruption. The highest honor? Any opportunity to work with Green Thumb and see his negotiation in action of course!
### Icon Relationships:
**Positive:** [[Radiant Commune of Mennatos]]. They both give each other EXACTLY what they need. Nobody has better blood than the bond Green Thumb built 25 years ago between these two.
**Negative:** [[Dwellers of the Deep]]. Direct competition and cultural differences makes these icons have difficult feelings. Is the antagonizing intentional by the Dwellers?
**Conflicted:** [[Fiefdom Afloat]]. It isn't clear if Green Thumb has his print on this deal. It's shaky, unstable, and better reflects rogue merchants than a mastermind of trade.
