Divine and Dwindling
### Icon: The Six Saints
![[Pasted image 20260514232813.png]]
Six fervent saints are the only remaining remnants of the old pantheon. Fien has expunged divine worship from their continent, pushing the Sainthood to wrestle with other icons for dominance. They differ in the gods and methods they worship...but unite under the goal of bringing godhood back into relevance. It's been a subtle infiltration. Each Saint has pushed their way to influence other icons, with many adopting their practices over the past century. All except the Baron. His close ties to Fien's Foreigners have made his people a tough nut to crack! But he isn't outright hostile; unlike the Foreigners who show merciless hostility at every encounter. Their fight for the dominance of science has carried over to this new continent! Everyone wonders how this conflict will affect the icons of the world. So abstract, so detached from more worldly concerns! Some believe that the Sainthood will help organize a divine society in the same way Fien has with its technology. Others see aspects, like their close relationship with the Creed, as omens of future disaster by messing with stones that shouldn't be uncovered.

**Quote:** "Gods of Secrets, of War, and many more. They once answered our prayers with power. Now? Silence. When the torch goes out, who protects us from the dark that closes in? I say the Sainthood shall bring light!"

**Usual Location:** Depends on the icon they follow! The great dwarf forge, the citadels of gold and crystal, and many more house the Six Saints. On important occasions, the Six gather at the Sacred Citadel.

**Common Knowledge:** Each Saint has inserted themselves among Icons that fit their worship. Saint Techne with the crafty dwarves, Saint Typhus with the Conqueror, and so on. It can be expected that Fien and the Sainthood butt heads everywhere they collide. But everyone wonders: if each Saint "plays nice" with other Icons, who do they truly align with? It's not 100% sure why, but the Sainthood has some odd ties with the Creed. Maybe even...friendly...behind closed doors. Perhaps the Sainthood sees some remnant of their old gods within the Creed. The Baron is a sharp enough man that he may see through the façade! Their widespread worship, strange ties behind closed doors, and fighting with Fien makes them something simple: disruptive. It's hard to tell if they have a "greater godly goal" or simple mortal bickering.

**Adventurer Quests:** This is HIGHLY dependent on the saint at the head of a quest. Some of them crave knowledge, worship, or communion with their gods. The one consistent goal between them all is to increase the presence of the gods on this continent. Smaller variations of this could be a simple church creation or divine tribute, while larger goals could include direct communication or even celestial summoning. Regardless, quests are flavored to the individual saint who gives them. Unless given as a collective...
### Icon Relationships:
**Positive:** [[Creed of the Contracted]]. Onir, the Man O' War, is said to be alive through the Creed's presence. An unhealthy obsession is present among the Sainthood.
**Negative:** [[Fien's Foreigners]]. After being forced out of Fien, the Sainthood sees them as ugly heretics. Deserving of nothing except excommunication and punishment for sins.
**Conflicted:** [[Crikh Barony]]. While the mortals of his realm find godhood appealing, the Baron is suspicious of being caught up in their abstract quarrel. He plays both sides as part of a game.
